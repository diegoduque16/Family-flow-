Family Flow privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. Family Flow is a private family calendar operated by Diego Duque (contact: diego.duque1984@gmail.com).

What we store

Your name, email, a salted hash of your password, and the profiles, events, tasks, lists and places your household adds. If you connect an outside calendar, copies of its events (title, time, location, description) are stored so they can be shown in your household's calendar.

Google and Microsoft calendar access

If you choose "Sign in with Google" or "Sign in with Microsoft", Family Flow requests read-only access to your calendars (Google: calendar.readonly; Microsoft: Calendars.Read). It uses this only to show your events inside Family Flow for your household. It never changes, deletes or shares your calendar events, never reads email, and does not sell data or use it for advertising or for training AI models. Access tokens are stored encrypted. You can disconnect at any time in Family Flow (More → Connected calendars) and revoke access in your Google or Microsoft account settings; disconnecting deletes the imported events.

Family Flow's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Assistant and voice

Messages you send to the assistant, with your household's upcoming events, are processed by Anthropic's Claude API to prepare suggestions. Voice recordings are transcribed by Cloudflare Workers AI and not stored.

Where data lives and how long

Data is stored with Cloudflare. You can export your household's data or delete your account or household at any time in the app (More → Your data / Account). Deleted data is removed from backups within 35 days.
